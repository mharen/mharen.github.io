iPhone + Apple Watch

Step Totals

Step Totals is a simple HealthKit-powered app for viewing daily steps, walking or running distance, and totals across a selected date range — on your iPhone, your Lock Screen, and your watch face.

What it does

  • Reads step count from Apple Health.
  • Reads walking or running distance from Apple Health.
  • Shows daily totals and totals across a custom date range.
  • Puts today's steps or distance on your Lock Screen and Home Screen with widgets.
  • Includes an Apple Watch app with watch-face complications.
